# 🏎️ Turbo Drift Racer

An arcade-style 2D highway racing game built with HTML5 Canvas, modern CSS, and procedural Web Audio API. Zero dependencies, instant loading, and runs anywhere!

---

## 🎮 How to Play

Simply double click or open `index.html` in your favorite web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, etc.).

### ⌨️ Keyboard Controls:
- **Steer Left / Right**: `◀` `▶` Arrow Keys or `A` / `D`
- **Accelerate**: `▲` Arrow Key or `W`
- **Brake / Reverse**: `▼` Arrow Key or `S`
- **Nitro Boost**: `SPACEBAR` or `SHIFT` (Hold to unleash hyper speed!)
- **Pause / Resume**: `P` or `ESC`

### 📱 Touch / Mouse Controls:
- On-screen touch buttons for Left, Right, Brake, and Nitro are provided automatically.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Dynamic Highway Racing**: 4-lane highway with animated lane markings, rumble strips, and roadside scenery.
- **Intelligent Traffic**: Multiple vehicle types (Sedans, Muscle Cars, Coupes, Semi-trucks, and lane-shifting Taxis).
- **Powerups & Collectibles**:
  - 🪙 **Gold Coins**: +100 bonus points.
  - ⚡ **Nitro Canisters**: Instantly restores 45% nitro boost fuel.
  - 🔧 **Repair Wrenches**: Heals your car hull by +30%.
- **Skill Moves & Combos**:
  - Close Call / Near Miss bonuses when speeding past traffic.
  - Skid marks on hard cornering at high speeds.
- **Procedural Sound Effects (Web Audio API)**:
  - Dynamic engine pitch shifts matching car speed and RPM.
  - Nitro boost whoosh, crash rumble, skid screech, and coin chime.
  - Mute/Unmute toggle with persistent preference.
- **Car Customization**: Choose between 4 paint schemes (Red Phoenix, Neon Cyber, Sunset Gold, Viper Violet).
- **Persistent High Scores**: Automatically saves your best score in local storage.
- **Day / Night Lighting Cycle**: Ambient lighting transitions with glowing vehicle headlights and taillights.
